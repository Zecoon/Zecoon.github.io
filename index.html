Hi! I’m Zekun (泽坤). I’m a graduate student at the Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR), Tsinghua University, and will continue my Ph.D. journey at Tsinghua.

I build LLM agents that actually do the work — searching, reading and reasoning — and I care about putting them where it matters, in medicine and education.

  • 🩺 Medical deep research. I lead TIMO, a family of small agentic models (4B / 27B, open-source release coming soon) that browse the web and reason over evidence to answer hard medical questions.
  • 🎓 Agents for education. Multi-agent systems that assess critical thinking from what students do, not only what they say, and that reimagine how people teach and learn online.
