I am a graduate student at the [Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR)](https://air.tsinghua.edu.cn/), Tsinghua University, and will continue my Ph.D. study at Tsinghua.

My research focuses on **LLM agents** and their applications in **medicine** and **education**:

- **Medical deep research agents.** I lead the development of [TIMO](https://huggingface.co/TIMO-OSS), an open-source lightweight agentic model family (4B / 27B) that searches, reads and reasons over the web to answer hard medical questions.
- **Multi-agent systems for education.** Interpretable multi-agent assessment of critical thinking, and agent-driven teaching and learning.
