**TIMO-OSS-Search-Preview** 是一组面向医学深度研究的开源轻量级智能体模型（4B / 27B）。模型会反复上网检索、阅读网页并基于证据推理，回答困难的医学问题。

- 模型：[huggingface.co/TIMO-OSS](https://huggingface.co/TIMO-OSS)
- 代码：[github.com/TIMO-Research](https://github.com/TIMO-Research)
- 入选第二届北京清华医学人工智能论坛（BEIHAI 2026）口头报告

<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">

**TIMO-OSS-Search-Preview** is an open-source family of lightweight agentic models (4B / 27B) for medical deep research. The model iteratively searches the web, reads pages and reasons over evidence to answer hard medical questions.

- Models: [huggingface.co/TIMO-OSS](https://huggingface.co/TIMO-OSS)
- Code: [github.com/TIMO-Research](https://github.com/TIMO-Research)
- Oral presentation at BEIHAI 2026 (2nd Beijing Tsinghua Medical AI Forum)

</div>
